## Limitations

- 21 cases is a small sample; the threshold is a reasonable setting for this corpus, not a proven optimum.
- Groundedness is judged by the same model family (Gemini); it is a second opinion, not ground truth. Read the answers above to confirm.
- EvaluationRunner retrieval hit rate is expected-document-in-top-5 over cases with an expected document; answer, refusal and clarification outcomes have separate expectations and denominators.
- Markdown documents have no page numbers, so they are cited by section; the expected page in `cases.json` is not checked.
- 10 cases in `eval/cases.json` (workflow, authorization, duplicate execution, agent failure, incomplete requests) do not apply to `/api/copilot/ask` and are not scored here.
