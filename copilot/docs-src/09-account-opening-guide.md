# Account Opening Guide

**Document ID:** BM-ACC-001 | **Version:** 1.0 | **Effective:** 2026-01-01 | **Owner:** Retail Accounts

*SYNTHETIC TRAINING DOCUMENT - for educational simulation only.*

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This guide explains how branch employees open personal accounts for walk-in customers. It covers the product range and minimum opening amounts, eligibility rules, the step-by-step opening procedure, the signature specimen card, the initial deposit, the welcome pack, joint accounts, and the special rules for minors who open a Youth Account. It applies to all branch employees in the retail banking area. The detailed document lists for each customer type are held in the Required Documents Checklist (BM-ACC-003), and interest rates are held in the Savings Products and Interest Rates document (BM-ACC-004). Maintenance and closure of existing accounts are covered in BM-ACC-002.

The guide does not cover loans, mortgages, investments, insurance, or corporate and business accounts. If a customer asks about those topics, the employee must tell the customer that this guide does not cover them and refer the customer to the Branch Manager.

## 2. Account Products and Minimum Opening Amounts

### 2.1 Product range

The branch offers five personal account products. The employee must match the customer's need to the correct product before starting the application.

- Basic Savings: an EGP savings account for everyday customers. The minimum opening deposit is EGP 1,000.
- Current Account: an EGP account with a cheque book facility. The minimum opening deposit is EGP 5,000, and the minimum balance is EGP 5,000. If the balance falls below EGP 5,000, a monthly fee of EGP 50 is charged.
- Salary Account: an EGP account for employees whose salary is transferred by an employer. There is no minimum opening deposit. The customer must provide an employer letter.
- Youth Account: a savings account for customers aged 12 to 17. A guardian is required for every Youth Account.
- USD Account: a United States dollar account. The minimum opening deposit is USD 500.

### 2.2 Choosing between products

A customer who wants to save money and earn interest is normally offered Basic Savings, or the Youth Account if the customer is aged 12 to 17. A customer who wants to write cheques is offered the Current Account. A customer whose salary will be paid by an employer is offered the Salary Account, but only after the employer letter has been seen. A customer who holds or expects to receive foreign currency is offered the USD Account. The employee must not promise interest rates or fees that are not written in the product documents.

## 3. Eligibility

### 3.1 General eligibility

An adult customer is eligible to open an account if the customer is at least 18 years old, can present the required identity documents, and is not on any internal blocking list. The employee must confirm that the person in front of the desk is the person shown on the identity document by comparing the photograph and the date of birth.

### 3.2 Residency

Egyptian residents must present a valid Egyptian national ID. Non-residents must present a valid passport plus a residence permit or visa. A non-resident customer may open Basic Savings or a USD Account. If the customer asks for any other product, the employee must refer the case to the supervisor before continuing.

### 3.3 Product-specific conditions

- Current Account: the customer must be able to deposit EGP 5,000 on the day of opening.
- Salary Account: the customer must provide an employer letter confirming employment and the salary transfer arrangement.
- Youth Account: the customer must be aged 12 to 17 and must be accompanied by a guardian.
- USD Account: the customer must be able to deposit USD 500 on the day of opening.

## 4. Opening Procedure

### 4.1 Step-by-step procedure

The employee must follow these steps in order for every new account.

1. Greet the customer, ask which product is needed, and confirm the eligibility rules in section 3.
2. Collect the original documents listed in BM-ACC-003 for the customer's scenario. At minimum these are a valid Egyptian national ID and a proof of address that is not older than 3 months.
3. Check that every document is valid and unexpired, and compare the photograph with the customer.
4. Enter the customer details into the core banking system exactly as they appear on the national ID. Check the spelling of the full name, the national ID number, and the date of birth.
5. Select the product and print the account opening form.
6. Ask the customer to read the form and sign it.
7. Ask the customer to sign the signature specimen card (see section 5).
8. Receive the initial deposit and issue the deposit receipt (see section 6).
9. Scan and save all documents to the customer file, and return the originals to the customer.
10. Hand over the welcome pack (see section 7) and explain the main fees and the monthly e-statement.

### 4.2 Supervisor review

After the account has been created, the supervisor reviews the file on the same day to check that the documents are complete and that the data matches the identity document. If the supervisor finds an error, the employee must correct it with the customer within 2 working days.

### 4.3 Incomplete applications

If a required document is missing or expired, the employee must not open the account. The employee must tell the customer exactly which document is needed and keep the application as a pending file for 5 working days. The employee must not accept photocopies in place of the originals.

## 5. Signature Specimen

Every new account requires a signature specimen card. The customer signs the card in front of the employee, using the signature that will be used on cheques and withdrawal slips. The employee must not sign or complete the card on behalf of the customer. The signature must be clear and must be written inside the box on the card. If the customer wants to change the signature in the future, a new specimen card must be signed in the branch with the national ID and the supervisor must approve the change.

For a joint account, every account holder signs a separate specimen card. For a Youth Account, the guardian signs a specimen card, and the minor also signs a specimen card.

## 6. Initial Deposit

The customer must pay the minimum opening amount of the chosen product on the day of opening, unless the product has no minimum. The deposit may be paid in cash at the teller or by transfer from another account in the customer's name. The employee must count the cash in front of the customer and issue a receipt. A cash deposit over EGP 100,000 requires the supervisor to confirm the source of funds before the account is activated. For the USD Account, the deposit is paid in United States dollars only. If the customer deposits less than the minimum, the account must not be activated, and the money must be returned or held as a pending deposit for 5 working days.

## 7. Welcome Pack

After the account is activated, the employee gives the customer a welcome pack. The pack contains the account number letter, the account terms leaflet, the signature specimen copy, and the debit card application form where the product allows a card. Card issuance fees are EGP 50 for Classic Debit and EGP 200 for Platinum Debit. The employee must explain that the customer is automatically enrolled for a free monthly e-statement, and that a paper statement costs EGP 20 per month. The employee must also tell the customer about the mobile app BM Digital, which requires the national ID, a registered mobile number, and an active debit card. If the customer wants the SMS alert service, the fee is EGP 5 per month.

## 8. Joint Accounts

A joint account is opened for two or more adult account holders. Every account holder must be present at the branch, must present the original documents, and must sign the application and a signature specimen card. The employee must record the operating rule agreed by the holders: either "either to sign" or "both to sign". If the customers do not state the rule, the default is "both to sign". The initial deposit and the minimum opening amount are the same as for a single account. A joint account cannot be closed unless the closure request is signed according to the operating rule, and the closure still needs supervisor approval.

If one of the joint holders is a non-resident, the supervisor must review the application before the account is activated.

## 9. Minors and the Youth Account

### 9.1 Age rules

The Youth Account is available only to customers aged 12 to 17. A child under 12 cannot open an account in a branch. When the holder reaches 18, the account must be converted to an adult product, and the employee must contact the customer to complete the adult documents.

### 9.2 Required documents and guardian

The guardian must be present at the opening. The employee must collect the minor's birth certificate and the guardian's valid national ID. The guardian must also provide a proof of address that is not older than 3 months. The guardian signs the application and the specimen card.

### 9.3 Operating rules

The guardian is responsible for the account until the minor reaches 18. The minimum opening deposit is EGP 1,000. The account cannot have an overdraft, cannot issue cheques, and cannot be used for a credit card. If the guardian wants to withdraw more than the amount agreed at opening, the employee must ask for the supervisor's approval. If the guardian's identity or legal authority is unclear, for example when the guardian is not a parent, the employee must stop and send the case to the supervisor and to Legal before opening the account.

## 10. Escalation

The employee must escalate to the supervisor in these cases: a non-resident asking for a product other than Basic Savings or a USD Account, a cash deposit above EGP 100,000, a doubtful identity document, a customer on a blocking list, or an unclear guardian relationship. The employee must never open an account first and correct the documents later.
