# Account Maintenance and Closure

**Document ID:** BM-ACC-002 | **Version:** 1.0 | **Effective:** 2026-01-01 | **Owner:** Retail Accounts

*SYNTHETIC TRAINING DOCUMENT - for educational simulation only.*

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This document explains how branch employees maintain personal accounts after opening and how they close them. It covers changes of customer details (address, phone number, name), dormant accounts and reactivation, statements and their fees, the minimum balance rule, the account closure procedure, the closure fee, the handling of a deceased customer, and the supervisor approvals that apply. It supports the Account Opening Guide (BM-ACC-001) and the Required Documents Checklist (BM-ACC-003). It does not cover loans, mortgages, investments, insurance, or corporate accounts.

## 2. Changing Customer Details

### 2.1 General rule

Before changing any customer detail, the employee must verify the customer's identity with the original national ID, compare the photograph with the customer, and record the request in the customer file. The employee must never change details on the basis of a phone call, a message, or a request from a third party.

### 2.2 Change of address

The customer must present the national ID and a new proof of address, which is a utility bill or a bank letter not older than 3 months. The employee updates the address in the core banking system, scans the proof of address, and saves it to the file. No fee applies. A change of address does not need supervisor approval. If the new address is outside Egypt, the employee must refer the case to the supervisor because the customer may now be classed as a non-resident, and the non-resident documents are then required.

### 2.3 Change of phone number

The registered mobile number is used for OTP messages, which are valid for 3 minutes, and for BM Digital login. Because of this, a change of the registered mobile number is a controlled action. The customer must come to the branch, present the national ID for in-branch verification, and sign the change form. The supervisor must approve the request before the employee executes it. The change takes effect within 1 working day after approval. A customer cannot change the registered mobile number by phone or through the mobile app. A change of a secondary landline number or of an email address is a normal update and does not need supervisor approval, but the identity check in section 2.1 still applies.

### 2.4 Change of name

A change of name, for example after marriage or a court order, requires a new national ID showing the new name, and the supporting official document such as the marriage certificate or the court decision. The employee must send the documents to the supervisor for review. After approval, the employee updates the name on all the customer's accounts and asks the customer to sign a new signature specimen card. If the customer has a debit card, a card with the new name can be issued as a replacement, and the replacement fee is waived only with a fee waiver approval under section 8.

## 3. Dormant Accounts

### 3.1 When an account becomes dormant

An account becomes dormant after 12 months with no customer-initiated transaction. Customer-initiated transactions are deposits, withdrawals, transfers, and card payments made by the customer. Interest credits, fees, and other bank-initiated entries do not count. On a dormant account, withdrawals, transfers, and card payments are blocked. Deposits into the account may still be received.

### 3.2 Reactivation procedure

To reactivate a dormant account, the customer must visit the branch with the original national ID. The employee verifies the identity, checks that the customer details are current, and submits the reactivation request to the supervisor. The supervisor must approve the request before execution. The reactivation is completed on the same day and no fee is charged. If the customer's address or phone number has changed, the employee must update it during the same visit by following section 2.

If the customer's national ID has expired, the employee cannot reactivate the account until the customer presents a valid ID. If the customer cannot visit in person, the employee must not reactivate the account.

## 4. Statements

### 4.1 Statement types and fees

- The monthly e-statement is free and is sent to the customer automatically.
- A paper statement costs EGP 20 per month.
- A duplicate statement copy costs EGP 30 per statement.

### 4.2 Handling statement requests

The customer chooses between e-statement and paper statement at account opening and may change the choice by signing a request in the branch. The employee must tell the customer the fee before issuing a paper statement or a duplicate copy. The fee is debited from the customer's account on the day of issue. If the account balance is not enough to cover the fee, the employee must ask the customer to deposit funds or must not issue the statement.

### 4.3 Fee waivers on statements

A supervisor may waive up to EGP 200 per request, which covers any normal statement fee. For example, if a customer disputes the EGP 30 duplicate fee because of a bank error, the supervisor may waive it. Any waiver above EGP 200 per request goes to the Branch Manager, who is outside this system.

## 5. Minimum Balance Rule

The Current Account has a minimum balance of EGP 5,000. If the balance is below EGP 5,000, a monthly fee of EGP 50 is charged. The employee must explain this rule to every customer who has or wants a Current Account. Basic Savings, Salary, Youth, and USD accounts do not charge a monthly fee for low balance, but the interest-earning products need a minimum balance to earn interest, as described in BM-ACC-004. If a customer asks for the EGP 50 monthly fee to be waived, the employee must raise a fee waiver request to the supervisor, and the employee must not reverse the fee without approval.

## 6. Account Closure

### 6.1 Closure procedure

The employee must follow these steps to close an account.

1. Verify the customer's identity with the original national ID.
2. Obtain the customer's written closure request, signed by the customer in the branch. For a joint account, the request must be signed according to the operating rule recorded at opening.
3. Check that all fees are settled, including monthly fees and any statement or card fees. Check that there are no pending transactions, standing orders, or cards linked to the account.
4. Calculate the closure fee (see section 6.2) and tell the customer.
5. Submit the request to the supervisor. The supervisor must approve before the employee executes the closure.
6. After approval, block the debit card, cancel the cheque book, and pay out the remaining balance by cash or by transfer to another account in the customer's name.
7. Give the customer a closure confirmation letter and save a copy in the file.

Account closure is completed within 7 working days from the date of the complete request.

### 6.2 Closure fee

A closure fee of EGP 100 applies if the account is closed within the first 6 months after opening. After 6 months, closure is free. The fee is deducted from the balance before payout. A supervisor may waive the fee within the EGP 200 waiver limit when there is a bank error or a documented customer hardship.

### 6.3 When closure cannot proceed

If the account has an unpaid fee that exceeds the balance, a legal hold, or a pending dispute, the closure must not proceed. The employee must explain to the customer what must be resolved first. If the account is dormant, the employee must first complete the reactivation in section 3.2, and then the closure request can follow, in the same visit if the supervisor approves both.

## 7. Deceased Customer

If an employee learns that an account holder has died, for example from a relative or from a notice, the employee must not make any decision about the account. The employee must not close the account, release funds, give out balance information, or change the account details. The employee must record what was reported, tell the customer or relative that the case will be handled by the proper department, and refer the case to Legal and to the supervisor immediately. Legal decides what documents are required and how the account is treated. For a joint account, the case is also referred to Legal, and the surviving holder must not be given funds until Legal confirms.

## 8. Supervisor Approvals

The following actions need supervisor approval before execution: change of registered mobile number, dormant account reactivation, account closure, and fee waiver. In this document, card replacement for a name change also needs approval. A supervisor may waive up to EGP 200 per request. A waiver above EGP 200 goes to the Branch Manager. The employee must record the name of the approving supervisor and the date of approval in the customer file. An approval given verbally without a record is not valid.

## 9. Escalation

The employee must escalate to the supervisor if the customer's documents are doubtful, the customer cannot attend in person for a controlled action, the customer disputes a fee above EGP 200, the customer reports a death, or the customer complains about service. A complaint must be acknowledged within 2 working days and resolved within 15 working days.
