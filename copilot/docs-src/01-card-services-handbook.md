# Card Services Handbook

**Document ID:** BM-CRD-001 | **Version:** 1.0 | **Effective:** 2026-01-01 | **Owner:** Card Services

*SYNTHETIC TRAINING DOCUMENT - for educational simulation only.*

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This handbook describes the card products offered by the bank to retail customers and sets out the rules that branch employees must apply when they issue, activate, renew, control or explain a card. It covers debit cards and credit cards, their limits, fees, PIN rules, expiry and renewal, credit card billing basics and the card controls that customers can use. It does not cover lost, stolen or damaged cards, which are handled in the Lost and Stolen Card Procedure (BM-CRD-002), card disputes, which are handled in the Card Dispute and Chargeback Procedure (BM-CRD-003), or ATM and POS faults, which are handled in the ATM and POS Troubleshooting Guide (BM-CRD-004). The employee must read this handbook together with those documents when a customer request spans more than one topic.

## 2. Card Types

### 2.1 Product Range

The bank issues four card types: Classic Debit, Platinum Debit, Standard Credit and Gold Credit. Debit cards are linked to a customer account and spend only the money available in that account. Credit cards are not linked to the account balance and spend from an approved credit limit. All four card types are valid for 4 years from the month of issue.

### 2.2 Debit Card Limits

The Classic Debit card has a daily ATM withdrawal limit of EGP 15,000 and a daily POS purchase limit of EGP 50,000. The Platinum Debit card has a daily ATM withdrawal limit of EGP 30,000 and a daily POS purchase limit of EGP 100,000. Daily limits reset at midnight. The ATM limit and the POS limit are counted separately, so a customer who has reached the ATM limit can still pay at a POS terminal up to the POS limit.

### 2.3 Credit Card Limits

The Standard Credit card is issued with a credit limit starting from EGP 10,000. The Gold Credit card is issued with a credit limit starting from EGP 50,000. The exact limit is set by the credit assessment team after the employee submits the application, and the employee must never promise a specific limit to the customer. Credit cards always require proof of income.

### 2.4 Card Fees

The issuance fee is EGP 50 for the Classic Debit card and EGP 200 for the Platinum Debit card. The annual fee is EGP 60 for Classic Debit, EGP 250 for Platinum Debit, EGP 300 for Standard Credit and EGP 800 for Gold Credit. Withdrawing cash at another bank's ATM costs EGP 8 per withdrawal, and a balance inquiry at another bank's ATM costs EGP 3. The optional SMS alert service costs EGP 5 per month. The employee must tell the customer about the fees before submitting the request.

## 3. Card Issuance

### 3.1 Eligibility and Required Documents

A debit card can be issued to any customer who holds an active account. For every card request the employee must see the customer's valid Egyptian national ID. A non-resident customer must present a valid passport plus a residence permit or visa instead. For a credit card the customer must also provide proof of address, which is a utility bill or a bank letter not older than 3 months, and proof of income, which is a salary certificate or the last 3 payslips. A credit card application without proof of income must not be submitted.

### 3.2 Issuance Steps

1. The employee verifies the customer's identity against the original ID and checks that the account is active and not dormant.
2. The employee confirms the card type with the customer and explains the issuance fee, the annual fee and the limits.
3. The employee enters the request in the card system and attaches scanned copies of the required documents.
4. The customer signs the application form and the fee is debited from the customer's account.
5. The employee gives the customer the request reference number and explains when and where the card will be collected.

New debit cards are normally ready at the branch within 5 working days. Credit cards depend on the credit assessment and the customer is informed of the result by SMS.

### 3.3 Card Collection

The customer must collect the card in person and show the original ID. The card is handed over in a sealed envelope and the PIN is set by the customer, never by the employee. The employee must not write down, ask for or repeat a PIN at any time. If the customer does not collect a card within 30 days, the branch returns it to Card Services for secure storage.

## 4. Activation

A new card must be activated within 60 days of the issue date. A card that is not activated within 60 days is automatically cancelled, and the customer must apply for a new card and pay the issuance fee again. The customer can activate a card in either of two ways. The first way is to change the PIN for the first time at any of the bank's ATMs. The second way is to activate the card inside the BM Digital mobile app. The employee cannot activate a card on behalf of the customer at the counter, but the employee should guide the customer to an ATM in the branch if the customer needs help.

## 5. PIN Rules

### 5.1 PIN Format

The card PIN is exactly 4 digits. The customer chooses the PIN at the first ATM PIN change. The employee must advise the customer not to use a birth year, a phone number digit sequence or repeated digits such as 1111.

### 5.2 Wrong PIN Attempts and Unblocking

Three consecutive wrong PIN attempts block the card. A blocked card cannot be used at ATMs or POS terminals until it is unblocked. Unblocking requires the customer to prove identity either in the branch with the original ID or through the BM Digital mobile app. The employee must not unblock a card over the phone or for a person who is not the cardholder. If the customer has forgotten the PIN, the employee raises a PIN reissue request under the CARD_SERVICE category, and the customer sets a new PIN at an ATM after the card is re-enabled.

## 6. Renewal and Expiry

### 6.1 Automatic Renewal

Each card is valid for 4 years. The bank starts the renewal process automatically about 60 days before the expiry date, provided the account is active and, for credit cards, the account is in good standing. The renewed card is sent to the home branch and the customer is informed by SMS. The old card stops working on its expiry date even if the new card has not been collected yet, so the employee should encourage customers to collect the new card early.

### 6.2 Exceptions to Renewal

A debit card is not renewed automatically when the linked account is dormant or closed. A credit card is not renewed automatically when the account has been more than 90 days overdue. In these cases the customer must visit the branch and the employee must raise a manual request. A dormant account must be reactivated first, which needs supervisor approval.

### 6.3 Expired Cards

An expired card cannot be reactivated. The employee must not cut the card unless the customer asks for it. If the customer needs a card urgently and the renewed card has not arrived, the employee raises a request under the CARD_SERVICE category and informs the customer of the 5 working day service level.

## 7. Credit Card Billing Basics

### 7.1 Statement Cycle

A statement is produced once a month on the statement date. It lists every purchase, cash advance, fee and payment since the previous statement, together with the total balance, the minimum payment and the payment due date. The payment due date is 21 days after the statement date. Statements are sent as free e-statements. A paper statement costs EGP 20 per month.

### 7.2 Minimum Payment

The minimum payment is 5% of the outstanding statement balance. For example, if the statement balance is EGP 8,000 the minimum payment is EGP 400. Paying the minimum keeps the account in good standing, but the unpaid part of the balance remains on the card and the customer should be told that it carries a monthly finance charge as shown on the statement.

### 7.3 Late and Over-Limit Fees

If the minimum payment is not received by the payment due date, a late payment fee of EGP 150 is charged. If the balance goes above the approved credit limit, an over-limit fee of EGP 100 is charged. The employee may explain these fees but may not remove them. A fee waiver needs supervisor approval. A supervisor may waive up to EGP 200 per request. Any request above EGP 200 goes to the Branch Manager. As an exception, no waiver can be offered if the same fee has already been waived once in the previous 6 months.

## 8. Card Controls

Customers can manage their card controls in the BM Digital mobile app at any time. The available controls are temporary freeze and unfreeze, online purchases on or off, international usage on or off, and ATM withdrawals on or off. A temporary freeze is not the same as a block. A frozen card can be unfrozen by the customer without visiting the branch, while a block for loss or theft is permanent and leads to a replacement card.

### 8.1 Limit Changes

A customer may request a temporary or permanent change to the daily ATM or POS limit within the maximum of the card type. A request to increase a limit needs supervisor approval before the employee executes it, and the change takes effect within 1 working day. The employee cannot raise a limit above the maximum of the card type. If the customer wants a higher limit than the card allows, the employee may suggest an upgrade from Classic Debit to Platinum Debit, with the issuance fee that applies.

### 8.2 Customer Reminders

The employee should remind every customer never to share the PIN or the OTP with anyone, including bank staff, to keep the SMS alert service active, and to report any card problem immediately through the Contact Center hotline 16555 or the mobile app.
