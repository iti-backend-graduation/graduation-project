# Digital Banking User Guide - BM Digital and Online Banking

**Document ID:** BM-DIG-001 | **Version:** 1.0 | **Effective:** 2026-01-01 | **Owner:** Digital Channels

*SYNTHETIC TRAINING DOCUMENT - for educational simulation only.*

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This guide explains how customers use the BM Digital mobile app and the online banking website, and how branch employees help customers who need assistance. It covers registration, first login, viewing balances, transfers, bill payments, beneficiary management and notifications. Security rules such as OTP, lockout and device binding are described in the document Digital Banking Security and Authentication (BM-DIG-002). Transfer types, fees and cut-off times are described in the Remittances and Transfers Guide (BM-DIG-004). Common problems are answered in the Digital Banking Support FAQ (BM-DIG-003).

The guide applies to individual retail customers only. The employee must use it as the first reference before contacting Digital Support.

## 2. Registration

### 2.1 Requirements

A customer can register for BM Digital only if all of the following are true. The customer holds a valid Egyptian national ID, or for non-residents a valid passport. The customer has a mobile number registered on the bank's system. The customer has an active debit card, meaning the card has already been activated and is not blocked or expired.

If the mobile number held by the bank is out of date, the customer cannot receive the registration OTP. The employee must start the mobile number change procedure described in BM-DIG-002 before registration can continue.

### 2.2 Registration Steps

The customer registers by following these steps in the app or on the online banking website.

1. Download BM Digital from the official app store of the phone, or open the online banking website.
2. Select "Register" and enter the national ID number.
3. Enter the debit card number and the card PIN when requested.
4. Enter the OTP sent by SMS to the registered mobile number. The OTP is valid for 3 minutes.
5. Create a username and a password. The password must have at least 10 characters including at least one letter, one digit and one symbol.
6. Accept the terms and conditions and confirm. The profile is created immediately.

The employee may guide the customer through these steps but must never type the PIN, the OTP or the password on behalf of the customer, and must never ask the customer to say them aloud.

### 2.3 Assisted Registration at the Branch

If the customer is not comfortable using a phone, the employee can offer a branch tablet in the digital corner. The customer enters all secret information personally. The employee only explains the screens. After registration the employee asks the customer to log out of the tablet and confirms that no data is stored on the device.

## 3. First Login

After registration the customer logs in with the username and password. On the first login on a new phone, the app sends an OTP by SMS to the registered mobile number. The OTP is valid for 3 minutes. The app then offers to bind the phone to the profile and to enable biometric login with fingerprint or face recognition. A profile may have a maximum of 2 bound devices.

If the customer enters the wrong password 5 times, the profile is locked for 24 hours. The employee must warn customers who are guessing a forgotten password to stop after the third attempt and use the "Forgot password" option instead.

Passwords expire every 180 days. The app shows a reminder 14 days before expiry. When the password has expired, the customer must choose a new one at the next login. The new password cannot be the same as any of the last 3 passwords.

## 4. Viewing Balances and Statements

### 4.1 Balances

After login, the home screen lists all accounts held by the customer, including Basic Savings, Current, Salary, Youth and USD accounts. The customer taps an account to see the available balance, the current balance and the last 20 transactions. The balance shown is updated in real time for instant and card transactions. Local transfers that are in progress appear as "pending" until they are settled.

A customer who prefers privacy can use the "Hide balance" switch on the home screen, which masks the balance until the customer taps it.

### 4.2 Statements

The customer can download the monthly e-statement from the app at no charge. A paper statement costs EGP 20 per month. A duplicate statement copy costs EGP 30 per statement. If the customer asks for a paper statement or duplicate, the employee raises the request in the branch and informs the customer of the fee before processing. Fee waivers need supervisor approval, and the supervisor may waive up to EGP 200 per request.

## 5. Transfers

### 5.1 Types of Transfer in BM Digital

BM Digital supports transfers between the customer's own accounts, transfers to other Banque Misr customers, local transfers to accounts in other banks, and instant transfers. International transfers are not available in the app and must be requested at the branch, as described in BM-DIG-004.

### 5.2 Limits

The transfer limit is EGP 50,000 per transaction and EGP 100,000 per day for each profile. Instant transfers have a lower limit of EGP 30,000 per transaction. The daily limit of EGP 100,000 counts all outgoing transfers and bill payments made the same day, from midnight to midnight. Transfers between the customer's own accounts do not count toward the daily limit.

The employee cannot raise these limits on behalf of the customer. If the customer needs a higher amount in a special case, the employee must advise the customer to split the amount across days or to complete the transfer at the branch.

### 5.3 Making a Transfer

1. The customer selects "Transfer" and chooses the source account.
2. The customer selects a saved beneficiary or adds a new one.
3. The customer enters the amount and an optional reference note of up to 35 characters.
4. The app shows a summary with the fee, if any. The customer reviews it carefully.
5. The customer confirms with the password or biometric login, and for new beneficiaries or high amounts with an OTP.
6. The app shows a confirmation screen with a reference number. The customer can save or share the receipt.

### 5.4 Cooling-Off Period for New Beneficiaries

A newly added beneficiary has a 24-hour cooling-off period for transfers above EGP 10,000. During this period the customer can still send EGP 10,000 or less to the beneficiary, but any transfer above EGP 10,000 is rejected with a message that the beneficiary is not yet eligible. The rule protects customers from fraud. The employee cannot bypass the cooling-off period under any circumstance, even when the customer is known to the branch or shows ID. If the customer needs to pay a larger amount urgently, the employee explains that the customer should wait until the 24 hours have passed.

## 6. Bill Payments

The customer selects "Bills" and chooses a category such as electricity, water, gas, mobile phone, landline, internet or school fees. The customer enters the subscriber number, and the app retrieves the amount due from the service provider. After confirming with password or biometric login, the payment is made immediately from the selected account.

Bill payments count toward the EGP 100,000 daily limit. The customer can save a biller for repeat payments. The service charge for bill payments is EGP 2 per bill. A payment that was successful on the app is final and the bank cannot cancel it. If the provider does not credit the payment, the customer must keep the reference number, and the employee raises a DIGITAL_BANKING service request.

## 7. Beneficiary Management

### 7.1 Adding a Beneficiary

The customer opens "Beneficiaries", selects "Add" and enters the beneficiary name, account number or IBAN, and bank. An OTP is sent to the registered mobile number to confirm. A customer can save up to 50 beneficiaries.

### 7.2 Editing and Deleting

The customer can edit the nickname of a beneficiary at any time. Changing the account number is not allowed. The customer must delete the beneficiary and add a new one, which starts a new 24-hour cooling-off period. Deleting a beneficiary does not affect transfers that are already pending.

## 8. Notifications

BM Digital sends free push notifications for every login, transfer, bill payment, password change and device change. The customer can turn on the paid SMS alert service, which costs EGP 5 per month and sends the same events as text messages, which is useful for customers who do not have data access. The employee must remind customers that notifications are a security tool: any notification about an action the customer did not make must be reported immediately according to BM-DIG-002.

## 9. How Employees Help Customers

The employee follows these rules when assisting a customer with digital banking.

- Verify the identity of the customer with the original national ID before discussing any digital banking profile.
- Guide only. The employee never asks for, writes down or keeps the password, PIN or OTP of the customer.
- Check the basic items first: the registered mobile number, the debit card status, the app version and the phone connection.
- Use the branch digital corner when the problem can be shown on screen.
- Where the problem is a system error or an unknown case, raise a DIGITAL_BANKING service request and give the customer the reference number.
- Fee waivers and profile unlocks need supervisor approval. Limits, cooling-off periods and OTP validity cannot be changed by any employee.
