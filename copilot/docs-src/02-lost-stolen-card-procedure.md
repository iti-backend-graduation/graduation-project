# Lost and Stolen Card Procedure

**Document ID:** BM-CRD-002 | **Version:** 1.0 | **Effective:** 2026-01-01 | **Owner:** Card Services

*SYNTHETIC TRAINING DOCUMENT - for educational simulation only.*

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This procedure tells branch employees exactly what to do when a customer reports that a debit card or credit card is lost, stolen, damaged or retained by an ATM. It explains how to verify the customer, how the card is blocked, how a replacement is requested and approved, which fees apply, how long delivery takes, who is liable for transactions, and when a case must be escalated as suspected fraud. It applies to all four card types: Classic Debit, Platinum Debit, Standard Credit and Gold Credit. Disputes about individual transactions are covered separately in the Card Dispute and Chargeback Procedure (BM-CRD-003), and general card rules are covered in the Card Services Handbook (BM-CRD-001).

## 2. Key Principles

### 2.1 Block First

The customer must block the card immediately. A card can be blocked at any time through the Contact Center hotline 16555, which is available 24 hours a day and 7 days a week, or through the BM Digital mobile app. Blocking does not need a branch visit. If a customer comes to the branch or calls the branch to report a lost or stolen card, the employee must first help the customer block the card and only then continue with the replacement request. The employee must never delay a block in order to complete paperwork.

### 2.2 Blocking Is Permanent

A block for loss or theft is permanent. A blocked card cannot be unblocked or used again, even if the customer finds it later. This is different from a temporary freeze in the mobile app, which the customer can undo. The employee must explain this difference before the block is confirmed, unless the customer is certain the card is lost or stolen, in which case the block must go ahead without further discussion.

### 2.3 Never Ask for the PIN

The employee must never ask for, write down or repeat the card PIN or an OTP, even when the customer offers it. The employee must also never ask the customer to read out the full card number over the phone. The last 4 digits and the customer's national ID number are enough to find the card record.

## 3. Verifying the Customer's Identity

Before any replacement request is raised, the employee must verify the identity of the person who is asking. The customer must show the original valid Egyptian national ID. A non-resident customer shows a valid passport plus a residence permit or visa. The employee compares the photo and name with the account record and checks that the person is the cardholder. A request made by a third party is not accepted, even if the third party holds a power of attorney, unless the Branch Manager has approved it in writing. If the customer cannot present an ID because it was lost together with the card, the employee asks the customer to bring a police report or an official replacement certificate, and the block is still done immediately. The replacement request waits until the identity is verified.

## 4. Step-by-Step Procedure for a Lost or Stolen Card

1. The employee asks the customer when and where the card was last seen, and records the approximate date, time and place of the loss or theft.
2. The employee confirms that the card is blocked. If the customer has not yet blocked it, the employee blocks it through the card system or calls the hotline 16555 with the customer, and writes down the block reference number.
3. The employee verifies the customer's identity as described in section 3.
4. The employee asks the customer whether any unfamiliar transaction appeared on the account or the SMS alerts after the card was last in the customer's hands, and lists the transactions the customer does not recognise, with dates and amounts.
5. The employee checks whether the case needs fraud escalation under section 8. If so, the employee follows section 8 before continuing.
6. The employee raises a replacement request in the card system under the CARD_SERVICE category and attaches the scanned ID.
7. The employee explains the replacement fee, the delivery time and the collection rules to the customer.
8. The employee submits the request to the supervisor for approval. The request is not executed until the supervisor has approved it.
9. After approval, the employee gives the customer the request reference number and confirms in writing the date the new card is expected.

## 5. Replacement Request, Approval and Fees

### 5.1 Supervisor Approval

Card replacement needs supervisor approval before execution. The supervisor checks that the block is recorded, that the identity was verified, that the correct card type is selected and that the fee is correct. The supervisor either approves or rejects the request in the card system on the same working day. A rejected request returns to the employee with a reason, and the employee informs the customer.

### 5.2 Replacement Fee

The replacement fee is EGP 100 for a debit card and EGP 150 for a credit card. The fee is debited from the customer's account when the request is approved. A replacement card is always issued with a new card number and a new expiry date counted from the issue month, so the card is valid for 4 years from that date. The customer sets a new PIN at the first ATM PIN change, and the new card must be activated within 60 days of issue or it is automatically cancelled.

### 5.3 Fee Waiver

The employee may not waive the replacement fee. A fee waiver needs supervisor approval. A supervisor may waive up to EGP 200 per request, which covers the full replacement fee for both debit and credit cards. As an exception, the supervisor should not waive the fee when the customer has already had a replacement in the previous 12 months, unless the fraud team has confirmed that the card was compromised at a merchant or an ATM. A waiver above EGP 200 goes to the Branch Manager, who is outside this system.

### 5.4 Delivery Time

The replacement card is ready in 5 working days from the date of supervisor approval. The card is collected at the home branch, and the customer must show the original ID at collection. The employee must tell the customer that the old card number will not work in the meantime, and that recurring online payments linked to the old card need to be updated with the merchants once the new card is active. For an urgent case the employee may explain that the customer can still use the BM Digital mobile app and the account at the branch while waiting for the card.

## 6. Liability for Transactions

### 6.1 After the Report

The customer is not liable for any transaction made after the card was reported lost or stolen. The time of the report is the time of the block recorded in the system or by the Contact Center, not the time the customer visited the branch. The employee must therefore record the block reference number and time on every request.

### 6.2 Before the Report

For unauthorised transactions made before the report, the customer's liability is capped at EGP 500, provided the PIN was not shared with anyone. The cap applies to the total of all unauthorised transactions on the card before the block, not to each transaction. If the unauthorised transactions total less than EGP 500, the customer bears only the actual total. As an exception, if the investigation finds that the customer shared the PIN, wrote it on the card or kept it with the card, the cap does not apply and the customer may be held responsible for the full amount. The employee must not tell the customer the final liability decision. The decision is made by the investigation team, and the employee only tells the customer that the cap of EGP 500 applies if the PIN was not shared.

## 7. Damaged Cards and Cards Retained by an ATM

### 7.1 Damaged Cards

A card that is cracked, bent, demagnetised or has an unreadable chip is a damaged card. A damaged card does not need to be blocked for loss. The employee verifies the customer's identity, collects the damaged card, cuts it in front of the customer and raises a replacement request in the same way as for a lost card. The replacement fee is EGP 100 for debit and EGP 150 for credit, and supervisor approval is needed. If the damage is proven to be a chip manufacturing fault, which the card team confirms after inspecting the card, the supervisor may waive the fee within the EGP 200 limit.

### 7.2 Cards Retained by an ATM

When an ATM keeps the card, the employee verifies the customer's identity and checks the ATM retention log. If the card is found in the ATM, ATM Operations collects it on the next business visit, and the branch cuts it and raises a replacement request. The customer can ask to keep the card block off until the card is recovered, but the employee must advise the customer that the safest choice is to block the card immediately. If the card was retained because of three wrong PIN attempts, the card is not lost and can be unblocked after the customer's ID is verified, once the physical card is recovered. If the card cannot be found in the ATM within 2 working days, the employee treats it as a lost card.

## 8. Fraud Suspicion and Escalation

The employee must treat the case as suspected fraud and escalate to the supervisor on the same working day when any of the following is true. The customer reports transactions that the customer did not make. The transactions total more than EGP 5,000 or happened in a foreign country. The card was used after being blocked. The customer says someone asked for the PIN or the OTP, or the customer received a suspicious call, SMS or link. The card was used at an ATM after it was reported as retained.

The supervisor reviews the details and forwards the case to the Fraud Monitoring team, which can place additional holds on the account. The employee must not discuss the investigation with the customer beyond the information in the case reference. The employee must also advise the customer to change the BM Digital password and to check the bound devices in the app. If the customer wants to dispute individual transactions, the employee follows the Card Dispute and Chargeback Procedure (BM-CRD-003).

## 9. Records and Customer Communication

The employee must record in the card system the date and time of the report, the block reference number, the way the card was lost, the transactions the customer does not recognise, the supervisor's approval and the fee charged. The employee confirms to the customer by SMS that the card was blocked. If the replacement card has not arrived after 5 working days, the employee escalates the request to Card Services and informs the customer of the new date. If the customer complains about the service, the employee acknowledges the complaint within 2 working days.
