# Customer Complaints and Escalation

**Document ID:** BM-CMP-001 | **Version:** 1.0 | **Effective:** 2026-01-01

*SYNTHETIC TRAINING DOCUMENT - for educational simulation only.*

## 1. Complaint registration

The employee records the customer's identity, contact number, subject, facts, and preferred contact method. The customer receives a complaint reference number.

## 2. Escalation

Complaints involving suspected fraud, discrimination, or a repeated unresolved issue are escalated to the supervisor on the same working day. Never promise a specific outcome or ask the customer for an OTP.
