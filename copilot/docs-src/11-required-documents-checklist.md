# Required Documents Checklist

**Document ID:** BM-ACC-003 | **Version:** 1.0 | **Effective:** 2026-01-01 | **Owner:** Compliance

*SYNTHETIC TRAINING DOCUMENT - for educational simulation only.*

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This checklist tells branch employees which documents must be collected for each common account and service scenario, how long a document stays valid, and what to do when a document is missing or expired. It applies to account opening, credit card applications, salary accounts, and the main maintenance requests. The employee must always check the original document, compare the photograph with the customer, and scan a copy to the customer file. The checklist does not cover loans, mortgages, investments, insurance, or corporate accounts.

## 2. Document Validity Rules

### 2.1 General rules

- The Egyptian national ID must be valid and not expired. An expired ID is not accepted for any request.
- A proof of address is a utility bill or a bank letter. It must not be older than 3 months on the day of the request, must show the customer's name, and must show the current address.
- Proof of income for a credit card is a salary certificate or the last 3 payslips. A salary certificate must be dated within the last 3 months.
- A passport used by a non-resident must be valid. A residence permit or visa must also be valid on the day of the request.
- The employer letter for a Salary Account must be on company letterhead, signed and stamped, and dated within the last 3 months.
- A signature specimen card is required for every new account and must be signed in front of the employee.

### 2.2 Originals and copies

The employee must see the original of every document. Photocopies and photographs of documents on a phone are not accepted. The employee returns the originals to the customer after scanning.

## 3. Scenario 1 - New Adult Resident Customer

The customer is at least 18 years old and lives in Egypt. The employee must collect the following.

1. A valid Egyptian national ID.
2. A proof of address (utility bill or bank letter) not older than 3 months.
3. A signed signature specimen card.
4. The initial deposit for the chosen product (EGP 1,000 for Basic Savings, EGP 5,000 for a Current Account, USD 500 for a USD Account).

No proof of income is needed for Basic Savings or a Current Account.

## 4. Scenario 2 - Minor (Youth Account)

The customer is aged 12 to 17, and a guardian must be present. The employee must collect the following.

1. The minor's birth certificate.
2. The guardian's valid Egyptian national ID.
3. The guardian's proof of address, not older than 3 months.
4. A signature specimen card signed by the guardian, and one signed by the minor.
5. The initial deposit of EGP 1,000.

If the guardian is not a parent, the employee must refer the case to the supervisor and to Legal before accepting the application.

## 5. Scenario 3 - Non-Resident Customer

The customer lives outside Egypt. The employee must collect the following.

1. A valid passport.
2. A residence permit or visa that is valid on the day of the request.
3. A proof of address not older than 3 months. The address may be abroad.
4. A signed signature specimen card.
5. The initial deposit for the chosen product.

A non-resident may open Basic Savings or a USD Account. For any other product, the case goes to the supervisor. An Egyptian national ID is not asked from a non-resident who does not hold one.

## 6. Scenario 4 - Credit Card Applicant

The customer applies for a Standard Credit card (credit limit from EGP 10,000) or a Gold Credit card (credit limit from EGP 50,000). The employee must collect the following.

1. A valid Egyptian national ID.
2. A proof of address not older than 3 months.
3. Proof of income: a salary certificate or the last 3 payslips.
4. The signed card application form.

If the customer has no proof of income, the employee must not submit the application. The employee tells the customer that proof of income is mandatory for every credit card. If the customer does not hold an account in the branch, the account opening documents in Scenario 1 are also required.

## 7. Scenario 5 - Salary Account

The customer wants a Salary Account, which has no minimum opening deposit. The employee must collect the following.

1. A valid Egyptian national ID.
2. A proof of address not older than 3 months.
3. An employer letter confirming employment and the salary transfer arrangement.
4. A signed signature specimen card.

If the employer letter is missing, the employee must not open a Salary Account. The customer may open Basic Savings instead, if the customer wishes, and the product can be changed later when the letter is available.

## 8. Scenario 6 - Mobile Number Change

The customer wants to change the registered mobile number. The employee must collect the following.

1. The original valid national ID, for in-branch ID verification.
2. The signed change form with the new mobile number.

The request needs supervisor approval before execution and takes effect within 1 working day after approval. No proof of address is needed. The request cannot be taken by phone, email, or from another person.

## 9. Scenario 7 - Dormant Account Reactivation

The account has had no customer-initiated transaction for 12 months. The employee must collect the following.

1. The original valid national ID.
2. The reactivation request form signed by the customer.

The supervisor approves the request, and the account is reactivated on the same day with no fee. A proof of address is needed only if the customer's address on file is older than 12 months or the customer says that the address has changed. In that case, the employee follows Scenario 8.

## 10. Scenario 8 - Address Change

The customer wants to update the address on file. The employee must collect the following.

1. The original valid national ID.
2. A new proof of address (utility bill or bank letter) not older than 3 months.

No fee applies and no supervisor approval is needed. If the new address is outside Egypt, the supervisor must review the request because non-resident documents may be required.

## 11. Missing or Expired Documents

### 11.1 Missing document

If a document is missing, the employee must not start or complete the request. The employee must tell the customer exactly which document is needed, give the checklist for the scenario, and offer to keep the application as a pending file for 5 working days. The employee must not accept a promise to bring the document later as a substitute.

### 11.2 Expired document

If a document is expired, for example an expired national ID or a proof of address older than 3 months, the document is treated as missing. The customer must bring a valid replacement. A receipt for a renewal application of a national ID is not accepted unless the supervisor approves it in writing.

### 11.3 Unreadable or doubtful document

If a document is unreadable, damaged, or looks altered, the employee must not accept it and must refer it to the supervisor. The employee must not accuse the customer. The employee only states that the document cannot be accepted.

## 12. Escalation Conditions

The employee must escalate to the supervisor in the following cases.

- The document appears forged, altered, or does not match the customer.
- The customer is a non-resident requesting a product other than Basic Savings or a USD Account.
- The guardian of a minor is not a parent.
- The customer asks the branch to accept an expired document as an exception.
- The customer cannot provide the documents after the 5 working day pending period and wants to extend it.

For a request in the ADDITIONAL_DOCUMENTS category, the employee records which documents are still outstanding and tells the customer the deadline. Only the supervisor or Compliance can approve an exception, and the employee must record the approval in the customer file.
