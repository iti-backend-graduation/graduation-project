# Savings Products and Interest Rates

**Document ID:** BM-ACC-004 | **Version:** 1.0 | **Effective:** 2026-01-01 | **Owner:** Retail Products

*SYNTHETIC TRAINING DOCUMENT - for educational simulation only.*

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This document describes the three interest-earning personal products offered by the branch: Basic Savings, the Youth Account, and the USD Account. It gives the features, the annual interest rates, the way interest is calculated and paid, the minimum balance needed to earn interest, and guidance on how to explain these points to customers. Account opening steps are in BM-ACC-001, and maintenance and closure are in BM-ACC-002. This document does not cover loans, mortgages, investments, or insurance.

## 2. Product Features

### 2.1 Basic Savings

Basic Savings is an EGP savings account. The minimum opening deposit is EGP 1,000. The customer can deposit and withdraw at any branch, and a debit card can be linked. The monthly e-statement is free.

### 2.2 Youth Account

The Youth Account is for customers aged 12 to 17 and a guardian is required. The minimum opening deposit is EGP 1,000. The account has no overdraft, no cheque book, and no credit card. At age 18 the account is converted to an adult product.

### 2.3 USD Account

The USD Account is held in United States dollars. The minimum opening deposit is USD 500. Deposits are accepted in United States dollars only.

## 3. Interest Rates

The rates below are annual rates (per annum, p.a.) and apply from the effective date of this document. The employee must quote the rate only as written here.

| Product | Annual rate | Payment frequency | Minimum balance to earn interest |
|---|---|---|---|
| Basic Savings | 9.0% p.a. | Quarterly | EGP 1,000 |
| Youth Account | 10.0% p.a. | Quarterly | EGP 1,000 |
| USD Account | 1.5% p.a. | Annually | USD 500 |

The Current Account and the Salary Account do not earn interest.

## 4. How Interest Is Calculated

Interest is calculated on the daily closing balance. For each day, the system takes the closing balance, multiplies it by the annual rate, and divides the result by 365. The daily amounts are added together and credited at the end of the payment period. For Basic Savings and the Youth Account, interest is credited at the end of each quarter. For the USD Account, interest is credited once a year.

For example, a Basic Savings account that holds EGP 10,000 for a full year at 9.0% p.a. earns about EGP 900 before any tax. In one quarter, the same balance earns roughly EGP 222 because the quarter has fewer days. If the balance changes during the period, each day is calculated on the balance of that day, so a deposit starts earning from the day after it is made. A withdrawal reduces interest from the day it is made.

## 5. Minimum Balance to Earn Interest

Interest is earned for a day only if the closing balance on that day is at least the minimum balance. For EGP products, the minimum is EGP 1,000. For the USD Account, the minimum is USD 500. If the balance is below the minimum on a given day, no interest is earned for that day, but the days when the balance is at or above the minimum still earn interest. This is a conditional rule, and the employee must explain it clearly because customers often think that interest is lost for the whole period.

## 6. Tax Note

Interest income may be subject to tax under the applicable law. The employee must not give tax advice or state a tax rate. The employee should tell the customer that any tax is deducted or reported as the law requires, and that the customer should ask a tax adviser for personal questions. The interest shown in the statement is the amount credited by the bank.

## 7. Explaining the Products to Customers

### 7.1 Key messages

- Interest is paid on the daily balance, so keeping the balance above the minimum every day matters.
- Basic Savings and the Youth Account pay interest quarterly, and the USD Account pays once a year.
- The rates are annual. A customer who receives interest every quarter does not receive the full annual rate at once.
- The rate quoted is the rate at the effective date and is shown in the product document.

### 7.2 Common customer questions

If a customer asks why the quarterly interest looks small compared with the quoted annual rate, the employee explains that the rate is annual and the credit covers one quarter only. If a customer asks which product is better, the employee compares the rates and the currency, and reminds the customer that the USD Account earns interest in dollars. If the customer asks about products outside this document, for example investments, the employee must say that the information is not available in this guide and refer the customer to the Branch Manager.

### 7.3 What the employee must not do

The employee must not promise a future rate, because rates are subject to change under a new version of this document. The employee must not guarantee a specific amount of interest, must not compare the bank's rates with another bank, and must not waive the minimum balance. A fee waiver does not change the interest rules.
