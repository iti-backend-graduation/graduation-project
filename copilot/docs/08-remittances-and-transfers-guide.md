# Remittances and Transfers Guide

**Document ID:** BM-DIG-004 | **Version:** 1.0 | **Effective:** 2026-01-01 | **Owner:** Payments Operations

*SYNTHETIC TRAINING DOCUMENT - for educational simulation only.*

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This guide explains the three transfer types offered to retail customers: local transfers, instant transfers and international transfers. It sets out limits, fees, cut-off times, the beneficiary data required, the treatment of rejected or returned transfers and the cooling-off rule for new beneficiaries. It applies to transfers made through BM Digital, online banking and the branch. It does not cover loans, investments or corporate payments.

## 2. Transfer Types at a Glance

| Type | Where available | Limit | Cut-off time | Settlement |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Local transfer | App, online banking, branch | EGP 50,000 per transaction, EGP 100,000 per day | 3:00 PM on working days | Same working day if before cut-off, otherwise next working day |
| Instant transfer | App and online banking | EGP 30,000 per transaction | None, available 24/7 | Within seconds |
| International transfer | Branch only | See section 5 | 12:00 noon on working days | 2 to 5 working days |

The daily limit of EGP 100,000 applies to all outgoing digital transfers and bill payments of a profile together.

## 3. Local Transfers

### 3.1 Rules

A local transfer sends Egyptian pounds to an account in Egypt, at Banque Misr or another bank. The limit is EGP 50,000 per transaction and EGP 100,000 per day. The cut-off time is 3:00 PM on working days. A transfer confirmed after 3:00 PM, or on a Friday, Saturday or public holiday, is processed on the next working day. The customer is told the expected settlement date on the confirmation screen.

### 3.2 Fees

Transfers between accounts at Banque Misr are free. Local transfers to other banks cost EGP 5 per transfer when made in the app and EGP 10 per transfer when made at the branch.

## 4. Instant Transfers

An instant transfer reaches the beneficiary within seconds, at any hour of the day, including weekends and holidays. The limit is EGP 30,000 per transaction, which is lower than the local transfer limit, and the transfers still count toward the daily limit of EGP 100,000. The fee is 0.5% of the amount, minimum EGP 3 and maximum EGP 20. An instant transfer cannot be cancelled once the customer confirms it. If the customer wants to send more than EGP 30,000, the customer should use a local transfer before 3:00 PM or split the amount, provided the daily limit is respected.

## 5. International Transfers

### 5.1 Availability and Fee

International transfers are made at the branch only and are not available in BM Digital. The fee is 0.25% of the amount, minimum EGP 150, maximum EGP 600, plus a SWIFT charge of EGP 100. For example, on a transfer of EGP 40,000 the percentage is EGP 100, so the minimum of EGP 150 applies and the total is EGP 250. On a transfer of EGP 400,000 the percentage is EGP 1,000, so the maximum of EGP 600 applies and the total is EGP 700. The employee must tell the customer the total fee before the customer signs the request.

### 5.2 Conditions

The customer must show the original national ID, or for non-residents a valid passport, and must state the purpose of the transfer. The employee processes only amounts up to EGP 250,000 per request. A request above EGP 250,000, or one with an unclear purpose, must be referred to Payments Operations and the employee cannot decide it. Requests received after 12:00 noon are processed on the next working day.

### 5.3 Foreign Currency

Transfers from a USD Account are sent in US dollars. Transfers from an Egyptian pound account are converted at the bank's selling rate on the day of processing. The customer is told that intermediary banks may deduct their own charges, which the bank cannot control.

## 6. Required Beneficiary Data

For a local or instant transfer the customer must provide the beneficiary's full name, account number or IBAN and bank name. For an international transfer the customer must provide the beneficiary's full name as it appears on the account, the IBAN or account number, the SWIFT/BIC code of the beneficiary bank, the bank name and country, and the beneficiary's address. If any item is missing or invalid, the transfer is rejected. The employee must check the data twice with the customer because the bank is not liable for transfers sent to account details given wrongly by the customer.

## 7. Rejected and Returned Transfers

### 7.1 Rejected Transfers

A transfer is rejected before it is sent when the amount is above the limit, the balance is insufficient, the beneficiary data are invalid or the cooling-off rule applies. Nothing is debited, and the customer is shown the reason.

### 7.2 Returned Transfers

A transfer may be returned after sending when the receiving bank closes the account or the account name does not match. For local transfers the amount is credited back to the customer's account within 3 working days of the return. For international transfers it is credited within 7 working days, and the SWIFT charge and intermediary bank charges are not refunded. The percentage fee is refunded only if the return was caused by a bank error. The employee must not promise a date and cannot decide the refund of any fee. A fee waiver of up to EGP 200 per request needs supervisor approval, and larger amounts go to the Branch Manager.

## 8. Cooling-Off Rule for New Beneficiaries

A newly added beneficiary has a 24-hour cooling-off period for transfers above EGP 10,000. During those 24 hours, transfers of EGP 10,000 or less are allowed, and larger transfers are rejected. The rule applies to local and instant transfers made in digital channels. It cannot be waived by an employee, and deleting and adding the beneficiary again starts a new 24-hour period. For international transfers made at the branch, the employee verifies the ID of the customer in person and the cooling-off rule does not apply.
