# Digital Banking Support FAQ for Branch Employees

**Document ID:** BM-DIG-003 | **Version:** 1.0 | **Effective:** 2026-01-01 | **Owner:** Digital Support

*SYNTHETIC TRAINING DOCUMENT - for educational simulation only.*

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This FAQ gives branch employees short answers to the most common BM Digital and online banking questions raised by retail customers. It complements the Digital Banking User Guide (BM-DIG-001) and the Digital Banking Security and Authentication Policy (BM-DIG-002). Where an answer says to escalate, the employee cannot decide the matter and must raise a DIGITAL_BANKING service request for Digital Support.

## 2. Login and Password Questions

### 2.1 What should I do when a customer forgot the password?

The customer selects "Forgot password" on the login screen, enters the national ID number and debit card number, confirms with the OTP sent by SMS and creates a new password. The new password must have at least 10 characters with at least one letter, digit and symbol, and must differ from the last 3 passwords. No employee action is needed.

### 2.2 The customer says the password stopped working after months of use. Why?

Passwords expire every 180 days. The customer must create a new password through "Forgot password" or at the next login prompt.

### 2.3 Why is the customer's profile locked and how long does it last?

5 failed login attempts lock the profile for 24 hours. The customer can wait, after which the profile opens by itself, or visit the branch with the original national ID so that a supervisor can unlock it after verification. The employee cannot unlock a profile alone. The full procedure is in BM-DIG-002 section 4.

### 2.4 Can I unlock a profile for a customer who phones the branch?

No. Unlocking requires in-branch ID verification and supervisor approval. Tell the caller to visit the branch with the original ID or wait for the 24 hours to end.

## 3. OTP Questions

### 3.1 The OTP did not arrive. What do I check?

Check that the registered mobile number on the profile is the number the customer is using, that the phone has signal, and that the SMS inbox is not full or blocking short-code senders. Remember that each OTP is valid for only 3 minutes and a new request cancels the old code. If the number held by the bank is wrong or the SIM was lost, start the mobile number change procedure.

### 3.2 How do I change the customer's registered mobile number?

The customer must come to the branch with the original national ID. The employee verifies the ID and raises the request, and a supervisor must approve it. The change takes effect within 1 working day after approval.

### 3.3 The customer read the OTP aloud to me. Is that allowed?

No. Employees must never ask for or accept an OTP. Tell the customer that the code is now unsafe and ask for a new OTP to be requested.

## 4. App and Device Questions

### 4.1 The app will not install. What can I suggest?

Ask the customer to download BM Digital only from the official app store, check that the phone has enough free storage, a recent operating system version and a working internet connection, and restart the phone. If the app store says the phone is not supported, or the problem remains after these checks, escalate to Digital Support with the phone model and operating system version. The employee cannot decide whether a specific phone model is supported.

### 4.2 How does a customer change to a new phone?

The customer installs BM Digital on the new phone, logs in with password and OTP and binds the new phone. A profile can have a maximum of 2 bound devices, so if two are already bound the customer must remove one first. If the old phone is lost, the customer visits the branch with ID so that a supervisor can have the old device removed.

### 4.3 Why can biometric login not be switched on?

Biometric login is available only on a bound device and only if the phone itself has a fingerprint or face setting. If the customer added a new fingerprint to the phone, the app disables biometric login until the password is entered again.

## 5. Transfer Questions

### 5.1 Why was the customer's transfer rejected?

Common reasons are listed below.

| Reason | Explanation |
|---|---|
| Amount above limit | EGP 50,000 per transaction, EGP 100,000 per day, EGP 30,000 per instant transfer |
| New beneficiary | Transfers above EGP 10,000 are blocked for 24 hours after the beneficiary was added |
| Insufficient balance | The available balance is less than the amount plus fee |
| Wrong beneficiary data | Account number, IBAN or bank is invalid |

### 5.2 Can I remove the 24-hour wait for a new beneficiary?

No. The cooling-off rule cannot be waived by an employee or a supervisor. The customer can send EGP 10,000 or less immediately, or wait 24 hours for a larger amount.

### 5.3 The customer sent money to the wrong account. Can the bank cancel it?

The employee cannot decide this. A completed transfer cannot be reversed by the branch. Escalate to Digital Support at once with the reference number, the amount, the time and the beneficiary details. Do not promise the customer that the money will be returned.

## 6. Other Questions

### 6.1 Can a customer have a higher transfer limit than EGP 100,000 per day?

The employee cannot grant this and the standard limits are not changed at branch level. Escalate to Digital Support if the customer insists, and meanwhile suggest splitting the payment across days or using the branch.

### 6.2 The customer sees a transaction they did not make. What now?

Treat it as suspicious activity. Verify the ID, tell the customer to reset the password and to call the Contact Center hotline 16555 immediately, raise an urgent DIGITAL_BANKING service request and inform the supervisor. Do not state who is liable.
